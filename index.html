Hallowed Heritage

The Spiritual Cradle of Sai Devotion in South India

Nestled in the historic and cultural heart of Mylapore, Chennai, the Sai Baba Temple at Alamelumangapuram is one of the most prominent, respected, and oldest Sai Baba temples across India. It serves as the world headquarters of the All India Sai Samaj (Regd.).

The temple owes its sacred inception to Pujyasri B. V. Narasimha Swamiji (1874–1956), an ardent apostle of Sai Baba. After experiencing divine transformation through Baba's grace, Swamiji traveled extensively throughout the subcontinent, recording first-hand experiences of Shirdi devotees and pioneering books like "Life of Sai Baba" and "Sai Satcharitra" interpretations.

In 1940, Swamiji established the All India Sai Samaj. Devotees subsequently purchased land in Mylapore, laying the foundation stone in 1949 and consecrating this sacred mandir in 1952. The temple welcomes millions each year without distinction of religion, caste, or creed, embodying Baba’s universal motto: "Sabka Malik Ek".

Sacred Fire of Purification

The Perpetual Akhanda Dhuni

At Shirdi, Sai Baba always sat before the Dhuni, distributing the sacred ash (Udi) which relieved devotees of their physical, mental, and spiritual distress. In Mylapore, Sri Narasimha Swamiji instituted the Akhanda Dhuni, keeping the sacred logs burning round the clock.

  • ✓ Devotees offer coconut pieces and wooden offerings directly to the sacred fire.
  • ✓ Holy Udi Prasad generated from this Dhuni is distributed freely to visitors.
  • ✓ Shrines of Lord Rama, Sita, Lakshmana, Hanuman, and Lord Ganesha surround the compound.

How to Reach the Mandir

Multiple convenient transit options across Chennai

🚆 Thirumayilai MRTS ~700m Walk

Just an 8-minute walk from Thirumayilai station (connects Beach & Velachery).

🚇 Chennai Metro ~2.5 km (Blue Line)

Nearest stations: AG-DMS or Teynampet (short auto/cab ride).

🚌 MTC City Bus ~300m Walk

Alight at Luz Corner or Mylapore Tank (frequent city-wide routes).

✈️ Chennai Airport (MAA) 14 km (~35 min)

Direct pre-paid taxis and app cabs easily reach via GST Road & Anna Salai.

Tip: Auto-rickshaws and shared rides are available round-the-clock outside Thirumayilai station gate.
