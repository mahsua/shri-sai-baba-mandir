About The Temple

A Legacy of Faith & Devotion

Established in 1968 and gracefully managed by the Shri Sai Bhakt Samaj (Regd.), Shri Sai Baba Mandir on Lodhi Road stands as one of the oldest and most revered temples dedicated to Shirdi Sai Baba in the National Capital Region.

Located near the Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium in Lodi Colony, the temple was built to serve as a peaceful spiritual haven for all devotees, regardless of religion, caste, or background. The central sanctum features a beautiful white marble idol of Sai Baba, elevated so devotees can seek blessings even while passing by.

Over the decades, it has evolved into both a central place of worship and a bustling center for social welfare, staying true to Baba's teachings of love and service.

Welfare

Community Seva

Inspired by Sai Baba’s teachings of Shraddha (Faith) and Saburi (Patience), the temple actively conducts daily charitable initiatives.

Daily Annakshetra

Serving freshly prepared, nutritious meals (Free Langar) to thousands of devotees, underprivileged individuals, and passersby every single day.

Healthcare Support

Organizing regular free health check-up camps, eye care and cataract drives, and distributing essential medicines to those in need.

Educational Assistance

Supporting underprivileged students by providing educational materials, books, uniforms, and financial aid for their continued learning.
